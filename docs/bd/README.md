# Stop selling APR before the cliff is understood.

![Abstract fixed-term credit note with one reference-price trigger](assets/one-loan-one-trigger.png)

**One borrower. One facility. One crash trigger.**

This field kit is for BD and credit conversations about the BTC barrier-note prototype. Start here
when the room needs the trade before it needs contract wiring.

The short version: investors fund a fixed-term borrower and sell a defined BTC downside payoff. The
borrower pays the facility first. Only then can a breached barrier create a cash rebate to the
borrower. If the borrower defaults, the BTC formula is off.

This is not an offer, price, tax view, suitability assessment or claim that a live series is ready.

## Pick your seat

| Seat | Plain-English read | First risk to say aloud |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Investor | “I lend to the borrower and sell BTC downside.” | The quoted return must pay for credit, option, liquidity, data and operations risk. |
| Borrower | “I pay more for debt that can rebate cash in a BTC crash.” | The rebate exists only after full facility performance. |
| Arranger | “I package one credit claim and one reference-price payoff.” | Exact data rights and observation rules are part of the product. |

Do not start with Wildcat unless the counterparty asks. For the first call, say “credit facility”,
“note issuer”, “investor” and “reference price”.

## Put numbers on it

Use [the $1m BTC worked example](worked-example.md) as the room script.

![Three-path barrier illustration](assets/barrier-cliff.png)

The example uses a `1,000,000 USDC` face amount, `100,000` BTC/USD strike, `60,000` barrier and
`1,030,000 USDC` collected-proceeds assumption. The one-unit move from `60,001` to `60,000` is the
teach: equality breaches, and the loss is measured from the strike.

## Keep it true

- Call it borrower credit plus sold BTC downside.
- Do not call the test APR an offered price.
- Do not describe principal as protected.
- Do not imply the borrower pays less into the facility when BTC falls.
- Keep the crash case and default case separate.
- Keep “research prototype; no external audit, legal approval or live series” on any extracted deck.

If a room cannot repeat the cliff and the no-rebate-on-default rule back to you, the next meeting is
still education, not terms.
