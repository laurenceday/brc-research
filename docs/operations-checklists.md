# BRC prototype operations checklists

These checklists apply only to the research prototype in this repository. They do not establish
that the contracts are audited, suitable for public distribution or safe for production capital.
The approved series manifest, legal terms and deployed contracts remain authoritative. If this
document conflicts with them, stop and escalate rather than improvising.

Complete one copy per series. Replace `<series-id>` in evidence names, record every transaction
hash and use UTC throughout. Store public evidence without wallet secrets, private RPC URLs,
personal data or unredacted legal material.

## Roles and escalation order

- **Release lead** owns the release gate and may declare a deployment blocker.
- **Security reviewer** owns technical finding disposition and blocks unresolved high-impact risk.
- **Borrower principal** controls the registered `BRCBorrowerAccount` and signs borrower actions.
- **Independent verifier** did not author the contracts or deployment scripts and works from a
  clean checkout and an independent RPC endpoint.
- **Funding operator** manages eligibility, subscription reporting, funding finalisation and
  activation readiness.
- **Market-risk monitor** watches Wildcat, sanctions, governance and borrower-identity state.
- **Maturity keeper** submits permissionless queue, withdrawal and settlement transactions.
- **Oracle evidence lead** assembles Chainlink round proofs and fallback evidence.
- **Ratifier** is one of the immutable ECDSA addresses named in the deployed oracle.
- **Ratifier coordinator** schedules ratifiers and relays approvals without replacing their judgment.
- **Incident commander** coordinates a suspected security or operational incident.
- **Communications lead** publishes approved factual updates.
- **Evidence custodian** preserves manifests, logs, signatures, receipts and investigation files.
- **Legal/compliance owner** decides legal, distribution, sanctions and disclosure questions.

Unless a row says otherwise, escalate first to the release lead before deployment and to the
incident commander after deployment. A role may have a named backup, but the evidence must say
who actually performed the step.

## 2. Independent fork rehearsal release gate

The independent verifier must perform this section. A contract or script author may observe but
must not operate the rehearsal or approve their own evidence.

| Done | Owner | When / time budget | Manual action | Required evidence output | Escalation route |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [ ] | Independent verifier | At the release commit; 4 hours | Create a fresh checkout, verify both pinned commits and run the complete unit, fuzz, stateful, differential and gas suites available in the repository. Record skipped tests and the known SphereX instrumentation diagnostic separately from actual failures. | `06-independent-test-run-<series-id>.log`, tool versions and exit codes. | Security reviewer and release lead; any unexplained failure or unavailable required suite blocks release. |
| [ ] | Independent verifier | Same session; 90 minutes | Fork the target chain at a recorded block, run the build preflight, deploy the series and run the read-only verifier using the candidate manifest. | Fork block, deployment transcript, manifest hash, predicted addresses and verifier success output. | Repository maintainer and release lead; an address or policy mismatch blocks release. |
| [ ] | Independent verifier | Same fork; 2 hours | Fund and activate the series, advance to maturity, call `queueSettlementWithdrawal` before waiting for `ST`, submit a valid candidate/predecessor round proof, call `executeSettlementWithdrawal` for each batch and `finalizeSettlement`, then exercise `claimBorrowerRebate` and `redeem`. | Transaction trace, relevant vault/oracle events and terminal accounting reconciliation. | Maturity keeper and security reviewer; any undocumented call ordering or gas gap blocks release. |
| [ ] | Independent verifier | Same fork; 2 hours | Repeat with delinquency and partial repayment: queue at maturity, enter recovery only after the contractual `recoveryDelay` while unpaid, execute available batches and finalise only after write-off eligibility and batch expiry. | Recovery trace, recovered-pool calculation and proof that borrower rebate is zero. | Market-risk monitor and security reviewer; unresolved value or timing gaps block release. |
| [ ] | Independent verifier | Same fork; 2 hours | Simulate primary-oracle unavailability. Exercise fallback proposal, threshold signatures, challenge/veto, primary cancellation of a pending fallback and successful finalisation after the waiting/challenge periods. | Proposal evidence hash, recovered ratifier addresses, event log and final fixing. | Oracle evidence lead and security reviewer; signature-domain or timing ambiguity blocks release. |
| [ ] | Independent verifier | Within 1 business day; 60 minutes | Write a short rehearsal report listing every command, manual judgment, delay, failure and workaround. State explicitly whether a non-author could execute the runbook without private assistance. | `07-fork-rehearsal-report-<series-id>.md`, signed by verifier and release lead. | Release lead; any unresolved execution gap is a deployment blocker. |

## 3. Deployment and independent verification

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [ ] | Release lead | T-60 minutes; 20 minutes | Recheck deadlines, Chainlink health, hook nonce, borrower principal, governance snapshot and gas conditions against the signed manifest. | Timestamped delta check at a named block. | Borrower principal and incident commander; material drift requires a new dry run and sign-off. |
| [ ] | Release lead | T-45 minutes; 20 minutes | Run `script/deploy-brc.sh` without `--broadcast` from the pinned clean checkout. | [ ] | Borrower principal | T-20 minutes; 15 minutes | Confirm the current principal-wide hook nonce still matches the manifest and approve the exact atomic `deploySeries` transaction. | [ ] | Borrower principal | T0; 15 minutes | Broadcast the atomic borrower-account deployment and wait for finality appropriate to the chain and value at risk. | Transaction hash, receipt, block number and `VaultDeployed`, hook and market deployment events. | Incident commander; on revert, preserve traces and do not change the signed manifest casually. |
| [ ] | Release lead | Within 15 minutes of finality; 15 minutes | Compare actual vault, oracle, hook, provider and market addresses with the dry run. | [ ] | Independent verifier | Within 30 minutes of finality; 30 minutes | From the clean checkout and independent RPC, run `VerifyBRC` against the approved manifest and generated record. | [ ] | Independent verifier | Same block range; 45 minutes | Read back `S0`, oracle metadata, borrower identity, market terms, hook flags, provider seal/lender, fees, governance/SphereX state and vault immutables. Confirm `pendingBorrower` is zero. | `08-post-deployment-readback-<series-id>.json`. | Relevant owner and incident commander; unexplained drift blocks funding. |
| [ ] | Evidence custodian | Within 1 hour; 30 minutes | Seal the manifest, record, build output, receipts, code hashes, readbacks and borrower sign-off into the series evidence pack. Record hashes in a separate append-only index. | `evidence-index-<series-id>.txt` and storage receipt. | Incident commander; missing evidence blocks funding even when chain state appears correct. |

## 4. Funding, finalisation and activation

| Done | Owner | When / time budget | Manual action | Required evidence output | Escalation route |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [ ] | Funding operator | Before eligibility changes; 30 minutes | Match the approved investor list and transfer policy to legal/compliance approval. Apply eligibility changes from the authorised access-controller account and review every emitted `EligibilitySet`. | Approved list reference, transaction hashes and event export. | Legal/compliance owner; discrepancies block subscriptions. |
| [ ] | Funding operator | At funding open; 15 minutes | Publish the deployed vault, settlement asset, `S0`, notional, hard cap, funding deadline, activation deadline and cancellation/refund route to approved participants. | Dated participant notice and content hash. | Communications lead and legal/compliance owner; correct factual errors before accepting funds. |
| [ ] | Funding operator | During funding; at least daily and after each material subscription; 20 minutes per reconciliation | Reconcile `Subscribed` events, note supply and vault settlement-asset balance. Confirm supply never exceeds notional and investigate transfer-fee or balance-delta failures. | Daily subscription ledger tied to block numbers. | Incident commander and security reviewer; unexplained imbalance blocks finalisation. |
| [ ] | Funding operator | At full raise or funding deadline; 20 minutes | If total supply equals the required full raise, call `finalizeFunding`. Otherwise call `cancelFunding` only after the deadline and publish refund instructions. | Transaction hash and `FundingFinalized` or `FundingCancelled` event. | Release lead; never force finalisation of a short raise. |
| [ ] | Independent verifier | After `Funded`, before activation; 30 minutes | Re-run the deployment verifier and live borrower-identity, hook, provider, fee, governance, SphereX, feed and market checks. Reconcile exact vault backing and note supply. | Pre-activation verification transcript at a named block. | Incident commander; any mismatch blocks activation and starts the cancellation assessment. |
| [ ] | Maturity keeper | Before activation deadline; 20 minutes | Call permissionless `activate` only after the pre-activation evidence is approved. Confirm the vault deposits exactly the notional and enters `Active`. | Transaction receipt and `Activated` event including market, hook, provider and initial fixing. | Incident commander and market-risk monitor; preserve a revert trace and do not retry blindly. |
| [ ] | Independent verifier | Within 20 minutes of activation; 20 minutes | Confirm vault settlement-asset delta, market-token/scaled balance, market supply, sole-lender provider and zero residual market allowance. | Post-activation balance and allowance snapshot. | Security reviewer; unexplained value or authority blocks further operational actions. |
| [ ] | Funding operator | At activation deadline if still `Funded`; 20 minutes | Call `cancelUnactivated`, verify `Cancelled`, and publish the permissionless holder refund path. Reconcile refunds until note supply reaches zero. | Cancellation receipt, refund ledger and remaining balance/supply report. | Incident commander and legal/compliance owner; activation failure is not permission to change terms. |

## 6. Maturity and normal settlement

| Done | Owner | When / time budget | Manual action | Required evidence output | Escalation route |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [ ] | Maturity keeper | T-72 hours; 90 minutes | Rehearse the exact live calldata on a fresh fork, including any authenticated pre-existing sanctions batch expiries. Reconfirm the safe 32-bit queue window. | Maturity fork transcript, gas estimates and proposed calldata. | Incident commander and security reviewer; an unsafe queue window triggers the external/legal contingency. |
| [ ] | Oracle evidence lead | T-24 hours; 30 minutes | Confirm independent round-data sources, phase handling and staff coverage. Prepare but do not guess candidate/predecessor IDs. | Readiness note and data-source links. | Incident commander; primary-feed uncertainty starts fallback evidence preparation, not early fallback submission. |
| [ ] | Maturity keeper | At or immediately after maturity; target 15 minutes | Call `queueSettlementWithdrawal` before waiting for `ST`. Supply at most eight unique authenticated pre-existing expiries when required. | Receipt and every `SettlementWithdrawalQueued` event, marking adopted versus new batches. | Incident commander and Wildcat operations; a missed safe queue window invokes the transaction/legal contingency because on-chain recovery may be unreachable. |
| [ ] | Oracle evidence lead | As soon as the first valid round is available; target 30 minutes | Identify the first valid round whose `updatedAt` is at or after maturity. Supply an earlier valid pre-maturity round so the contract can scan intervening rounds. For a phase transition, the predecessor must be the previous phase tail. Have the independent verifier reproduce the choice. | Candidate/predecessor proof bundle with raw RPC responses and timestamps. | Incident commander and ratifier coordinator; do not submit an ambiguous proof. |
| [ ] | Maturity keeper | Within 15 minutes of proof approval; 15 minutes | Submit `recordMaturityFixing(candidate, predecessor)` and confirm the stored round, price and time match the reviewed proof. | Receipt and `MaturityFixingRecorded` event. | Oracle evidence lead and incident commander; preserve reverts and evaluate fallback timing without altering evidence. |
| [ ] | Borrower principal | By the contractual repayment plan; 15 minutes per transaction and 10 minutes per daily reconciliation | Fund the Wildcat claim and close the market where practical. Closing an empty market is not a prerequisite for vault settlement. | Repayment/closure transaction hashes and updated batch state. | Market-risk monitor and incident commander; unpaid claims enter delinquency tracking. |
| [ ] | Maturity keeper | After each recorded batch expires; target 30 minutes | Call `executeSettlementWithdrawal` for withdrawable batches and reconcile received settlement assets. | Receipt, `SettlementWithdrawalExecuted` event and asset-delta calculation. | Incident commander and Wildcat operations; unexpected zero/partial execution enters recovery assessment. |
| [ ] | Maturity keeper | After complete Wildcat performance and valid `ST`; 30 minutes | Call `finalizeSettlement` and verify proceeds, slash, fixed borrower rebate and noteholder reserve against the independent payoff model. | `SettlementFinalized` event and signed settlement calculation. | Security reviewer and incident commander; accounting mismatch blocks rebate and redemption communications. |
| [ ] | Borrower principal | After settlement finalisation; 15 minutes | Call `claimBorrowerRebate` and verify that it pays the exact rebate only to the immutable borrower-principal recipient. | `BorrowerRebateClaimed` event and recipient balance delta. | Incident commander and legal/compliance owner; no rebate is available in recovery. |
| [ ] | Funding operator | After `Redeemable`; daily until complete; 15 minutes per review | Publish instructions for holders to call `redeem`, monitor `Redeemed` events and reconcile note burn, recipient payments, reserve and stated dust until `Settled`. | Redemption ledger and terminal reconciliation. | Incident commander and legal/compliance owner; do not promise assets outside the fixed pool. |

## 7. Delinquency, sanctions and recovery

| Done | Owner | When / time budget | Manual action | Required evidence output | Escalation route |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [ ] | Market-risk monitor | On first delinquency or partial batch; 15 minutes | Open a recovery watch, record debt/liquidity/batch state and notify the borrower principal. Keep the normal queue operation running at maturity. | Incident timeline and market snapshot. | Incident commander and borrower principal. |
| [ ] | Market-risk monitor | Before recovery eligibility; daily; 15 minutes per review | Distinguish delayed execution from an objectively unpaid queued claim. Confirm every known vault batch, including sanctions-created batches, is recorded. | Batch inventory with expiries, scaled amounts and paid amounts. | Security reviewer and Wildcat operations; incomplete batch provenance blocks recovery transitions. |
| [ ] | Legal/compliance owner | On sanctions alert; within 1 hour | Confirm whether the vault position is in sanctions escrow and approve factual communications. Do not claim the vault can bypass or seize escrowed assets. | Sanctions decision log and approved notice. | Incident commander and external counsel. |
| [ ] | Maturity keeper | At or after `recoveryEligibleAt`; 20 minutes | Call `enterRecovery` only if the complete queued claim remains partly unpaid. Confirm a fully paid claim cannot enter recovery. | Receipt, `RecoveryEntered` event and unpaid-claim proof. | Incident commander and security reviewer; a failed predicate remains on the normal path. |
| [ ] | Maturity keeper | Whenever a recovery batch becomes executable; 20 minutes | Call `executeRecoveryWithdrawal`, record the amount received and update the recovered-pool ledger. | Receipt, withdrawal event and balance delta. | Market-risk monitor and incident commander; investigate reverts or missing liquidity. |
| [ ] | Maturity keeper | At or after write-off eligibility and after every recorded expiry; 30 minutes | Recheck all batches, execute anything newly available, then call `finalizeRecovery`. Confirm the borrower rebate is zero and the terminal pool equals recovered assets. | `RecoveryFinalized` event and signed pool reconciliation. | Security reviewer and legal/compliance owner; premature or inconsistent finalisation is a critical incident. |
| [ ] | Funding operator | After recovery becomes `Redeemable`; daily until complete; 15 minutes per review | Publish the fixed recovered-pool redemption route and explain that later Wildcat payments or unsolicited transfers do not enlarge note claims. | Holder notice, redemption ledger and terminal reconciliation. | Communications lead and legal/compliance owner. |

## 8. Oracle fallback proposal, challenge and ratification

| Done | Owner | When / time budget | Manual action | Required evidence output | Escalation route |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [ ] | Oracle evidence lead | From maturity until fallback availability; at least hourly during an outage; 15 minutes per review | Keep attempting and documenting the primary candidate/predecessor proof. Calculate `fallbackAvailableAt` from deployed values rather than a spreadsheet alone. | Primary-attempt log and on-chain fallback time calculation. | Incident commander; fallback is not evidence that no unsubmitted primary round exists. |
| [ ] | Oracle evidence lead | Before proposal; 60 minutes | Assemble price, observation time, fixed source ID and public evidence. Hash the exact evidence package and have a second person reproduce it. | Immutable evidence bundle, URL/location, hash and independent check. | Ratifier coordinator and legal/compliance owner; unverifiable evidence blocks proposal. |
| [ ] | Ratifier coordinator | At or after fallback availability; 20 minutes | Select an available immutable ratifier to call `proposeFallback`. Confirm no conflicting active proposal and record its nonce and challenge deadline. | Receipt and `FallbackProposed` event. | Incident commander; a wrong proposal should be challenged, not informally edited. |
| [ ] | Each ratifier | During challenge period; 60 minutes per signer | Independently check the primary-round status, source evidence, chain ID, oracle address, series ID, proposal nonce, price, observation time and evidence hash before signing. | Individual signed review statement and signature with recovered address. | Ratifier coordinator; abstain and escalate any uncertainty. |
| [ ] | Ratifier coordinator | During challenge period; 15 minutes per approval | Submit valid approvals and reconcile unique immutable signers against the threshold. Never reuse a signature for a replacement nonce. | `FallbackApproved` events and threshold worksheet. | Incident commander and security reviewer; malformed or duplicate signatures are evidence, not approvals. |
| [ ] | Any ratifier | Until finalisation; target 15 minutes after detecting a problem | Call `challengeFallback` when evidence, source, price or process is unacceptable. Confirm the proposal cannot finalise. | `FallbackChallenged` event and challenge rationale. | Incident commander, legal/compliance owner and all ratifiers. |
| [ ] | Maturity keeper | While fallback remains pending; target 15 minutes after a valid primary proof appears | Submit the valid primary round proof. Confirm it records the primary fixing and cancels the pending fallback. | `MaturityFixingRecorded` and `FallbackCancelledByPrimary` events. | Oracle evidence lead and incident commander. |
| [ ] | Maturity keeper | After challenge period and threshold; 20 minutes | Recheck absence of a valid submitted primary fixing and ratifier veto, then call `finalizeFallback`. Confirm the stored fixing exactly matches the approved proposal. | `FallbackMaturityFixingRecorded` event and final comparison. | Incident commander and security reviewer; never retry with changed facts under the same nonce. |
